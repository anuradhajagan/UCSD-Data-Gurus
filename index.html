How have living conditions and development changed across countries from 1960 to 2020?

DSC 209R Project 3: Juan Mo, Nick Puckdee, Snigdha Tiwari, Anuradha Jaganathan

1. Family Size: Fertility Rate (1960–2019)

The average number of children born per woman from 1960 to 2019 decreased from about 5 to 2.5 showing that family sizes are getting smaller.

Source: World Bank

2. Internet Users by Country Income Level (~2000 vs ~2019)

Internet use grew in every income group from 2000 to 2019, but only about 14% of people in low-income countries were online (2017, the latest data) compared with 89% in high-income countries.

Light blue = ~2000; dark blue = ~2019. Hover over a bar to see the exact percentage and year.

Source: World Bank

3. Electricity Access change (2000 vs 2019).

Electricity access expanded between 2000 and 2019, but large gaps remained across sub-Saharan Africa..

Source: World Bank

4. Population Aging (1960–2020)

The proportion of people aged 65 and above has increased in most countries from 1960 to 2020, reflecting longer life expectancy and declining fertility rates.

Source: World Bank

5. Average Research and Development(R&D) Expenditure Across Countries

Average R&D spending as a share of GDP rose overall from 1996 to 2018, with fluctuations along the way.

Source: World Bank
