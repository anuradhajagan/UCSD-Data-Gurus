How have living conditions and development changed across countries from 1960 to 2020?

DSC 209R Project 3: Juan Mo, Nick Puckdee, Snigdha Tiwari, Anuradha Jaganathan

1. Population Aging Over Time (1960–2020)

The global share of people aged 65 and above increased from about 5% in 1960 to over 9% in 2020, showing that the world’s population is aging.

Source: World Bank

2. Family Size: Fertility Rate (1960–2019)

The average number of children born per woman from 1960 to 2019 decreased from about 5 to 2.5 showing that family sizes are getting smaller.

Source: World Bank

3. Global Access to Electricity (2000 vs 2019)

Electricity access improved in many countries from 2000 to 2019, but remained limited in parts of sub-Saharan Africa.

Source: World Bank

4. Internet Users by Country Income Level (~2000 vs ~2019)

Internet use grew in every income group from 2000 to 2019, but only about 14% of people in low-income countries were online (2017, the latest data) compared with 89% in high-income countries.

Source: World Bank

5. How Much Do Countries Spend on Research and Development? (% of GDP)

The average share of GDP spent on research and development among countries with available data fluctuated over time but generally increased after 2007.

Source: World Bank
